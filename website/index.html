01 THE IDEA

Your desktop,
with a softer edge.

Some days the desktop is a workspace. Other days it’s a pile of almost-finished thoughts. Carpet Studio gives the pile a place to rest.

01Place it anywhere

Move and resize the carpet to fit your desktop.

02Peek when you need to

Lift a corner and see what’s underneath.

03Gather, then restore

Optionally bring icons together and return them to their saved positions.

MAKE IT YOURS

A little character.
A lot of calm.

Four starting designs, with room for your own image. Choose the one that feels at home on your screen.

In the Mac app, you can also import your own rug image.
