CARPET STUDIO — INSTALLATION

1. Open the disk image.
2. Drag Carpet Studio.app to the Applications shortcut.
3. Eject the disk image, then open Carpet Studio from Applications.
4. Look for its rug icon in the menu bar. The first-run guide explains the controls.

The carpet covers desktop icons without moving files. Covering needs no special permission.
If you choose Gather Icons or Restore Icon Positions, macOS asks whether Carpet Studio may
control Finder. Allow Finder Automation for those commands. You can change this later in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

Before Gather Icons, turn off Finder's Use Stacks and choose Sort By > None on the desktop.
Carpet Studio saves icon positions before gathering and restores them from its menu or
when you quit normally.

MOTION PREVIEW 0.3.0
Pull the upper-right corner inward to lift the fabric. Release it to let it settle.
Space or Return holds the corner open when the rug has keyboard focus.
Drag the body to move; drag the lower-right corner to resize.
The app respects macOS Reduce Motion.
